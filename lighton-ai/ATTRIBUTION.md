## Cache provenance

FastEmbed 6.0.2 selects the repository and ONNX paths recorded in `models-manifest.toml`. If this
artifact is eventually published, redistribution is conditional on checksum verification and the
separate publication-time license/copyright/notice audit; no LightOn archive is currently
redistributed by the planned v6 release.
